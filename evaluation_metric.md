 ## Problem Grounding

 ### 1.
New Employees.

 ### 2.
Step-by-step training provided by supervisors or experienced employees using existing project materials

 ### 3.
Organizes materials and provides training without taking up experienced employees' time, allowing new employees to ask questions anytime, anywhere to learn about any topic or issue they are interested in.⁠

 ## Evaluation Plan Draft

 ### Success Definition:
Assess new employees' understanding of the topic through quizzes before and after using this tool.

 ### Target Users:
We can invite students with team software project experience, recent additions to new projects or teams, and students with internship experience or about to enter internships to participate in the test.

 ### Method:
First, administer a quiz on the learning topic to assess the new employee's level. Then, the new employee uses this tool to complete the course materials within a limited time and asks questions to the LLM. A second quiz is then administered.

 ### Minimum Evidence Threshold:
Comparing participants' pre- and post-test scores, points lost should be reduced by 20%, and the overall pass rate should reach above 80%.
